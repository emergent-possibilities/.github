## What We Do

We help founders, operators, and business owners figure out what to build, what to automate, what to avoid, and how to move forward with less uncertainty.

- **Technical strategy** — pressure-test product direction, architecture, vendor decisions, AI plans, and infrastructure choices before real money is spent.

- **Rapid prototyping** — waitlists, onboarding flows, AI workflows, internal tools, and investor demos real enough to learn from.
- **Team formation** — structure lean teams, evaluate engineers and vendors, define ownership, and create operating rhythm.
- **Practical support** — secure email, 2FA, vendor cleanup, operational security, and compliance prep for growing businesses.

## How We Think

The most valuable technical work often happens *before* code is written: identifying assumptions, understanding constraints, sequencing risk, and deciding what *not* to build.

We prefer:

- lean execution over process
- practical systems over slide decks
- shipping over meetings
- experienced judgment over unnecessary complexity

Some of our operating principles:

- **Build only after the goal is clear.** A prototype is only useful if it answers the right question.
- **Architecture is a business decision.** It reflects the market, team, regulatory environment, and cost of change — not just technology choices.
- **The riskiest assumption deserves the first proof.** We isolate the hard question and test it directly.
- **Complexity should be earned.** Premature complexity is usually just uncertainty made expensive.
- **The goal is not more software.** It's better leverage — sometimes that means a memo, a map, a vendor decision, or a reason not to build.
