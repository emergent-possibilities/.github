# Emergent Labs

A small lab of engineers and designers building practical tools, in the open where we can.

![Emergent Labs](./assets/hero-github-banner.png)

Emergent Labs is a small group of software engineers and designers. We explore new technology and build tools that solve practical problems: some for our own use, some with the teams we work alongside. We publish in the open where we can, collaborate closely rather than hand work off, and take on most new work by referral.

---

## Where we plug in

Most of the time we're heads-down building. When we do work with others, it's usually one of two conversations:

- **Founders & product teams — *building or scaling software?*** Apps, platforms, POCs, architecture, infrastructure, and technical leadership.
- **Small business owners — *wondering where AI actually fits?*** A one-day review of your workflows, tools, and the practical AI opportunities (and the parts best left alone).

---

## How we think

Same loop whether it's someone else's problem or our own:

- **Prove the problem.** Is it real, and is it worth solving? Most ideas die here, and that's fine.
- **Understand why it's a problem.** The obvious pain is rarely the root. Dig until the real constraint shows up.
- **Name the assumptions and constraints.** Technical, operational, financial — what's actually true vs. what we're guessing.
- **Build and iterate.** Ship something real enough to learn from, then let feedback drive the next move.

We prefer:

- lean execution over process
- practical systems over slide decks
- shipping over meetings
- judgment over unnecessary complexity
